Before-show checklist

Voice diagnostics

On the Mac running the Earshot host, choose Earshot → Open Logs Folder…, or Open Logs Folder… in the Earshot tray menu. Browser clients do not hold the host’s logs.

The default Mac folder is ~/Library/Logs/Earshot/. The current file is earshot.log; older files are earshot.1.log and earshot.2.log. If EARSHOT_DATA_DIR is set, logs go into its logs subfolder instead.

Capture a comms problem​

  1. Keep the current channel and People settings so the log reflects the problem.
  2. Let several people speak normally on the comms channel. Include a few longer replies if possible, and a clean PGM comparison.
  3. Open the logs folder and keep earshot.log. Include the rotated files if the relevant time has moved into them.
  4. Note the channel and approximate time of a reply that should have had a name. Send the log with that information when reporting the issue.

What the log explains​

Entries with the earshot_voice target share an utterance ID and channel ID, so the input, comparison, learning and final result can be followed together.

EventWhat it tells you
model_readyWhich voice model loaded, its execution provider, thread count and embedding size.
inputChannel name, sample rate, audio duration, RMS and peak levels in dBFS, clipping, invalid samples, and whether recognition, discovery and the model are enabled/installed.
speech_gateThe loud and noise RMS estimates, effective gate floor and accepted 20 ms frames. A long recording can still contain very little speech according to this gate.
comparisonDetected speech duration, availability of a usable observation, saved voice count, best and runner-up speaker IDs/scores, confidence margin, and the exact duration/score/margin thresholds for display and automations.
candidate_rejectedWhy an observation cannot enter the learning pool, including the required speech duration.
candidate_started / candidate_progressPending voice similarity, number of consistent observations, total speech, and the requirements to establish a voice.
candidates_expiredPending observations were discarded after two minutes without matching speech.
learning_not_saved / learning_save_failedAn observation did not qualify for saving, or saving failed.
decisionThe speaker selected for display and the separate, stricter automation result.
answerThe automation answer, including early exits such as recognition off, missing model, startup, missing audio or a full recognition queue.
name_published / name_publish_failedWhether a selected name was actually stored and announced to clients.

Very short replies​

Replies with less than one second of detected speech remain unnamed under the current matching rules. For diagnostics, discovery can extract an observation from at least 250 ms of detected speech, so its scores can be inspected, but that does not lower the naming or automation thresholds. The current learning pool still requires at least one second per observation.

This distinction matters on comms: “go” may contain only a few hundred milliseconds of usable speech even when its recorded segment is much longer. Single-word samples are not a reliable basis for claiming that two people are the same voice. Diagnostics expose the actual reason instead of assuming that every missing name is a model installation problem.

Log storage​

Logging is enabled by default, writes off the transcription thread and keeps at most three 10 MiB files. Its memory queue is bounded; extreme overload can drop log entries. A normal app shutdown records the dropped-entry count and flushes the writer.

Logs include channel names, IDs, timestamps, numeric signal measurements and decision scores. They do not record transcript text, raw audio, voice embeddings, stored voiceprints or credentials. Existing replay clips are separate from these logs.

Developers can override the filter with EARSHOT_LOG. The default is info,earshot_voice=debug; EARSHOT_LOG=warn suppresses normal voice diagnostics. Cloud HTTP request logging remains clamped to info so request URLs containing glossary terms are not exposed at trace level.
