Badges, marks and indicators

Earshot says a great deal with small labels. This page lists them all, by where you meet them. The wording is exactly what the app shows.

In the header​

The connection indicator​

It saysWhat it meansWhat to do
LiveThis screen is connected to the host.Nothing.
ConnectingThe page has just opened and is reaching the host.Wait a moment.
Reconnecting"Connection lost. Reconnecting (attempt N)."Wait. It keeps trying by itself and catches up on missed lines.
Engine unreachable"No connection to the engine. Retrying (attempt N)."Check this device's network, and that the host is running and awake.
DemoConnected, but to a demo show: a host started in demo mode ("The engine is running its demo show."), or the built-in mock ("Showing the built-in demo show, not a live engine.").Nothing, unless you expected a real show.

Safe and Armed​

It showsWhat it means
Safe (lit), with Hold to arm beside itAutomations that match are only logged. Nothing is sent. On a narrow screen the second button reads Arm.
ARMED in red, with Make safe beside it, a red band across the header and "ARMED" in the browser tab titleAutomations that match really send.
Arming…Your arm is on its way to the host.
Making safe…Your Safe is on its way to the host.
Safe? or ARMED? (with a question mark)"Safe — connection lost, state unknown" (or "Armed — …"). The screen has lost the host and is showing the last state it knew. Arming is not possible. Press Make safe anyway.
"Could not confirm Safe — the host may still be armed."Earshot could not confirm your Safe. Press Retry until it clears.
"The host is armed. Your Safe was overtaken — press Make safe again."Someone armed after your Safe. Press Make safe again.

A viewer sees Safe or ARMED as a display only, with no buttons. The whole subject is in Safe and Armed.

The display button​

It shows a language code (for example ES) while you are reading in another language.

On a channel​

These appear in the Feed's channel list, on lane headers, in Focus and in Setup. A channel that is listening normally shows no badge in the transcript views. In Setup, where the absence of a badge would be unclear, it shows Listening.

Channel state​

BadgeToneWhat it meansWhat to do
(none) / ListeningThe channel is hearing its input and transcribing.Nothing.
IdlegreyThe channel is switched off, or has not started.Check it is enabled under Setup, Channels.
StartinggreyThe channel is opening its input, or a linked channel is connecting.Wait.
PausedgreySomeone, or an automation, paused it. It is not listening.Resume it when you want it back.
Behindamber"Transcription is running behind; live drafts are paused until it catches up". Every line is still transcribed, but late.See below.
No inputredThe audio device is not delivering audio.Check the interface, its cable, and the operating system's microphone permission.
OfflineredA channel from another Earshot computer, whose computer cannot be reached. Earshot keeps retrying.Check the other computer and the network. Hover the badge for the detail.
ErrorredSomething went wrong. For a linked channel, the other computer refused.Hover the badge: the detail says what.
DisabledgreyIn Setup's channel list only: the channel is switched off and does not listen.Switch it on under Setup, Channels.

Hover any badge for the detail the host gave.

Behind comes on when the backlog on that channel goes over 3 seconds, and goes off when it falls under 1 second. While it is on: no draft text, final lines arrive late, cues set to fire on final text fire late, and lines get no names. Help is in Troubleshooting.

Where one lane or one Focus covers several channels (a group), the badge is that of the channel in the worst state.

The level meter​

The bar under a channel's name shows how loud its input is, on a scale from -60 dB to 0 dB, with a thin mark at the peak.

The meterWhat it means
Moves when someone talksThe input is alive.
Never movesWrong input number, a dead line, or no microphone permission.
Turns amberPeaks are within 6 dB of the top. Loud, still fine.
Turns redPeaks are within 1 dB of the top. The input is clipping: turn it down at the source, or lower Gain.

A channel from another Earshot computer has no meter, because no audio comes across.

Other marks on a channel​

MarkWhat it means
A numberLines you have not read on that channel ("unread lines" in the Feed, "new lines" on a lane).
Speaking nowLanes: someone is talking on it at this moment.
PinnedLanes: the lane is pinned to the left.
A cloud, reading CloudThe channel is set to cloud speech. Either lines are coming back from the cloud ("Cloud speech: audio from this channel is sent to Deepgram, outside this building, to be transcribed."), or that is simply how it is set ("Set to cloud speech: this channel’s audio may be sent to Deepgram, outside this building.").
A cloud, reading Cloud paused"Cloud speech is paused after failures. Lines are transcribed on this computer; Earshot tests the service with one short piece of audio about every 30 seconds."
A cloud, reading On this computerThe channel is set to cloud speech but is being transcribed locally, because "Deepgram rejected the key" or there is "No key saved". No audio is sent.
A link markThe channel comes from another Earshot computer. It says "From" and the address.
Not encryptedThat link is plain http: "Lines from this host travel unencrypted on the network."

People who cannot read diagnostics (viewers) only ever see the plain Cloud mark. See Cloud & audio and Showing another Earshot computer's channels.

On a transcript line​

MarkWhat it means
Soft grey textA draft: the person is still talking and the words may change. Screen readers hear "still speaking".
Solid textA final line.
A dimmed word with a dotted underlineEarshot was unsure of that word.
A name with a coloured dotEarshot recognised the voice. "Recognised by voice. May be wrong."
Typed, with a nameSomeone typed the line. Typed lines never fire phrase automations.
A small pencil"Changed from what was heard. Show the original." Operators and admins only.
A small play control"Play the audio of" the channel and time. The channel keeps its audio.
Original, before muted text under a lineYou are reading a translation, and this is what was actually said.
Translating…A translation is on its way (up to 10 seconds).
Not translatedNo translation arrived, or the line cannot be translated.

More on each in The screen at a glance.

In the Archive​

BadgeWhat it means
Live nowThe session that is running at this moment. Other sessions show how long they ran instead.
Highlighted words in a search resultThe words you searched for.

In Automations, the Test bench and Activity​

MarkWhereWhat it means
ProblemAutomations listThe automation cannot run, or needs fixing. The line under it starts "Not running:" or "Needs fixing:" and says what to change. See When an automation is not running.
Would have firedTest benchThe phrase matched. The test bench is always a dry run.
Really sentA firing's cardIt was a live firing, not a dry run.
Would run / Ran / SkippedBeside each action of a firingWhat that action did, or would have done. A skipped action gives its reason.
Dry runActivityThe automation matched while the show was Safe (or the firing was held back), so nothing was sent.
FailedActivityThe send was tried and did not succeed. Open the row for the reason.
Ignored for this triggerAutomation editor, on a conditionThe condition cannot apply to this kind of trigger.

See Run now, the Test bench and Activity.

In Setup​

BadgeWhereWhat it means
RecommendedModelsInstall this one.
InstalledModelsDownloaded, checked and ready. Before that a row may say "Verifying the download…".
In use nowNames on lines, Translate with, What incoming OSC may doThe choice in force, as opposed to the card you have picked but not yet applied.
Voice learnedPeopleEarshot has that person's voice.
No voice yetPeopleThe person exists but no voice has been learned. No name will appear for them.
Voice needs learning againPeopleA stored voice no longer fits the voice model. Learn it again.
PinnedGroupsThe group is listed first.
Password set / No password setAccessWhether a show password exists. Until one is set, nobody can join by password.
Key saved / No key / Checking…Cloud & audioWhether the host holds a key for that service. The key itself is never shown.
Working, Waiting, Paused, Key rejected, No key, Not usedCloud & audio, cloud speech statusHow cloud speech is doing. See Cloud & audio.
Encrypted / Not encryptedFrom another Earshot hostWhether that computer offers https.
Not connectedShow control, MIDI input portsA MIDI port you ticked is not plugged in at the moment.
"••••"Anywhere a secret is savedA key, code or password is saved. It cannot be read back. See Saved secrets.
