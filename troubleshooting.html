Two places to look first

The header of every screen says whether the screen is Live and whether the show is Safe or ARMED. Activity says what every automation did, or why it did not. Most answers are in one of those two places.

No text is appearing​

  1. Does the header indicator say Live? If not, the screen is not connected to the host. See A phone cannot connect.
  2. Is the channel enabled, and what does its badge say? No input, Offline, Error, Paused or Idle explain themselves. Hover the badge for the detail. See Status and badges.
  3. Is a speech model installed? Setup, Models should show Installed next to Parakeet and Silero VAD. See Models.
  4. Does the level meter move on that input? Look in Setup, Patch, or in the Channels list. If it does not move, the input number may be wrong, the device may not be delivering audio, or the operating system may have been denied microphone permission. Check the system's microphone permission for Earshot. See Patch and channels.
  5. Is the input too quiet? Raise Gain on the channel. If speech is still not picked up, look at Sensitivity under Advanced: speech detection. See Channels.
  6. Is the computer asleep? If the computer sleeps, Earshot stops listening.

The badge says Behind​

Behind means transcription on that channel is running late. Every line is still transcribed. Draft text stops, and final lines arrive later than usual. It comes on when the backlog on that channel goes over 3 seconds, and goes off when it falls under 1 second. See Reading the transcript.

To help it catch up:

  1. Turn off Show words as they are spoken on channels that do not need drafts. Drafts use more processing.
  2. Disable channels you are not using.
  3. Close other heavy programs on the host.

Remember that cues set to fire on final text fire later while a channel is Behind.

A cue did not fire​

Go down this list in order.

  1. Is the show Armed? In Safe nothing is sent. Open Activity: a row reading Would have fired Name with Dry run means it was matched and held back by Safe. See Safe and Armed.
  2. Was the show Armed from the moment the words began? Something said while the show is Safe never fires, even if you arm while Earshot is still working out the words. Activity says "heard before the show was armed" or "Safe was pressed after this was said". Arm first, then speak.
  3. Is the automation on, with no problem? Check the Enabled switch and look for a Problem badge or "1 automation is not running." See When an automation is not running.
  4. Did Earshot hear it correctly? Read the line in the Feed. Then type that line into the Test bench and see if the automation would fire. Remember it matches whole words, after numbers are turned into digits. See Run now, the Test bench and Activity.
  5. Was it refused for a number reason? Look in Activity for "Name" did not fire: the number may be incomplete: …. See How Earshot hears numbers, and say the number a different way.
  6. Cooldown. Activity says cooling down (N ms left). The default cooldown is 2 seconds.
  7. A condition. Activity says condition not met: …. Check channel, group, time window and flag conditions, and Where to listen. See Building an automation.
  8. The runaway stop. Look for the alert "fired 20 times in 10 seconds and was stopped".
  9. Was the line typed? Typed lines never fire phrase automations.
  10. Did it come from another Earshot computer? Lines from a linked channel never fire automations here.
  11. Is it limited to named people? It fires only when Earshot is very sure who spoke. Activity says why ("voice not recognised", "the voice was X, not Y", "no voice was heard for this trigger"). See Automations limited to people.
  12. Fire on Draft or Final. On Final text it waits for the settled line, and a channel that is Behind is slow.
  13. Did it fire, but the destination did not get it? Activity shows the send. A row marked Failed says why. Check the destination's host, port and protocol (under OSC destinations in the editor), and whether the device is on the network. A TCP destination that is down is retried, and the sends skipped meanwhile are logged. See Actions.

A cue depends on a word that is often mis-heard​

Automations hear what was said, not the corrected text. The glossary changes only what people read.

Add the mis-hearing to the automation's phrases. For example, if you want "alesha go" to fire a cue but the recogniser writes "aleesha go", add "aleesha go" as another phrase in the automation. See Triggers and Glossary.

Names are not appearing on lines​

  1. Setup, Models: is Voice recognition (CAM++) installed? The People screen says if it is not.
  2. Setup, People: does the person show Voice learned? "No voice yet" or "Voice needs learning again" means learn it.
  3. Setup, Channels: is Separate and recognise voices on this channel on for that channel? New audio channels start with it on; existing channels keep their previous setting.
  4. Is the line very short? Short lines get no name. A line needs about a second of real speech.
  5. Is the channel Behind, or did the model only just start? Lines get no name then.
  6. The voice may not be learned, or not learned on this channel. A name can also be missing because Names on lines is on Cautious.

See People.

A name keeps coming out wrong​

Add it to the glossary (Setup, Glossary) with the way it comes out under Sounds like.

Look for "Not applied:" on the entry: Earshot will not apply a correction that touches a number or a word an automation listens for. See Glossary.

A linked channel shows no lines​

Read the status line under the channel.

The status saysWhat to do
"Connecting to …"Wait. It is still trying.
"Cannot reach …: … Retrying."The other computer is off, or the network between the two is down. Earshot keeps trying by itself.
"The link code was refused. …"Save the channel again with the right code, or switch it off and on. A refused code is not retried, and fixing the code on the other computer alone does not bring the link back.
"… refused the link: …"Check the other computer still has Shared with other Earshot hosts on for that channel.
"Linked to …", but no linesNobody has spoken on that channel since the link came up, or the channel is paused here.

See Showing another Earshot computer's channels.

Lines are not translating​

  1. Is a language chosen under Read in (the display button in the header)? Are you on Feed, Lanes or Focus? Earshot asks the host to translate for you only on those three.
  2. Does the menu offer the language? It lists only what the host can translate into. An admin needs to install a pair under Setup, Models, or choose a cloud translator.
  3. If the host refused, the sentence under the display menu (and the bar under the header) says why.
  4. A line that says Not translated was not translated in time, or is in a language Earshot cannot translate from.

See Read in another language.

There is no play button on a line​

Only spoken lines from a channel with Keep audio for replay on get one, a moment after the line.

  • Lines from before it was switched on have none.
  • Typed lines have none.
  • If it says "This audio is no longer kept.", the retention limits deleted it.

See Replay a line's audio.

A phone cannot connect​

What the phone showsWhat to do
"This address isn't one the host recognises."Use the address under Setup, Access, Address for the crew.
"Can't reach the Earshot computer. Check your network and try again."Check the phone is on the same network as the host. The page keeps trying by itself.
The password page: "That password is not right."Check it. After five wrong tries a device is locked out: "Too many tries. Try again in m:ss." Wait.
"This show needs a personal link or QR code from the operator."The show is on Personal links. Make the person a link or invite.
"This code is not valid or has already been used. Ask the operator for a new one."An invite works once and lasts 10 minutes. Make a new one.
Signed in on a Home Screen app on iPhone, but Safari was used beforeSign-in does not carry over. Ask for a new code.
Everyone was working, then everyone is sent to a sign-in pageThe password was changed, the access mode changed, or the host restarted.
"Reconnecting" or "Engine unreachable" in the headerThe screen has lost the host. It keeps trying by itself. Check the host is running and awake.

See Who can connect, Password, Invites and personal links, Phones and tablets and What happens when the host restarts.

OSC is not arriving​

  1. Is Controllers may send OSC to Earshot on? New audio channels start with it on; existing channels keep their previous setting.
  2. Is the level high enough? Make safe only ignores everything except Safe, ping and version. Move to Trigger automations for triggers, or Full control to clear transcripts or arm.
  3. Is the sending device in Devices allowed to control? The list starts as "Only this computer". Add its IP address.
  4. For arming: it needs the level set to Full control, a TCP port set, and the sender connected by TCP. A UDP sender can never arm.
  5. Is the port right? UDP 4781 by default; TCP is empty until you set it.
  6. Look in Activity. Refused OSC is logged with the reason and a count.
  7. If you added a device list, is it a plain IP address (no port, range or name)?
  8. Is the address spelled exactly as in the address list? There are no aliases.
  9. Did a view control (select channel, scroll) do nothing? It only changes the Earshot window on the host computer, and not while someone is in Setup, Automations, Archive or Activity.
  10. Did an Arm do nothing? A packet that also contains a Safe never arms. Look in Activity for "Arm ignored".

To prove the path, send /earshot/ping and wait for /earshot/reply/pong. It works from any device at every level. See OSC examples and OSC input.

MIDI is not triggering​

  1. Is the port ticked under Setup, Show control, MIDI inputs? If the controller was plugged in after Earshot started, press Refresh.
  2. Does Activity show a "MIDI in" row when you press the control? If not, the message is not reaching Earshot.
  3. Do the message type, channel and number in the automation match what arrived?
  4. Is the show Armed? In Safe the row is a Dry run.

See MIDI.

Still stuck?​

  • Read Limits and known behaviour: what you are seeing may be a known gap, or something that has never been tried.
  • Start the host with more detail in its log: set EARSHOT_LOG=debug before starting it. Logs never contain what was said, or any secret. See earshotd.
  • Go through the before-show checklist from the top.

Voice names missing on comms​

Open Earshot → Open Logs Folder… on the host Mac and capture several replies. The voice diagnostics guide explains the recorded speech duration, signal levels, match scores and reasons for withholding a name. Very short replies may be below the naming and learning minimums even when transcription works.
