Cloud and audio

Open Setup, then Cloud & audio. The screen says what it is for: "Where audio and text may leave this computer, and how long saved audio is kept. Each cloud service is off until you choose it."

It has three parts:

PartWhat it controlsWhat leaves the building if you turn it on
Cloud speech (Deepgram)Speech recognition by an outside service, one channel at a time.That channel's audio, and your glossary words.
TranslationWhich translator turns lines into other languages, and how many languages at once.With DeepL or Claude: the text of each translated line. No audio.
Saved audioHow long replay audio is kept, and how much.Nothing. Saved audio stays on the host.

If you never choose a cloud option, no audio and no text goes to any company.

Who can do this

Only admins. Operators and viewers cannot open Setup. Viewers see only a "Cloud" mark on a cloud channel.

The mock show. The key shown as saved is a stand-in, the counters are invented, and the saved-audio limits are the mock's, not the real defaults.

Cloud speech (Deepgram)​

Cloud speech has never been run against the real Deepgram service

It has been tested against a stand-in server only, and some details of how Deepgram answers are unverified. Before you rely on it, try one channel with a real key. Whether the model-improvement opt-out that Earshot sends changes Deepgram's price is also unconfirmed.

What it is​

By default every channel is transcribed on the host computer by a local model. Cloud speech sends one channel's audio to Deepgram, a company outside the building, to be transcribed there instead.

  • It is chosen per channel, never for the whole show.
  • Only finished lines' audio goes to Deepgram. Drafts always stay on this computer.
  • Your glossary words are sent with it, as hints. They travel in the request address.
  • Every request carries Deepgram's model-improvement opt-out.

Turn it on for a channel​

  1. Save a key first (below), or the channel will carry on being transcribed locally.
  2. Open Setup, Channels, and the channel.
  3. Under Engine, choose Deepgram (cloud).
  4. Earshot asks "Send this channel's audio to Deepgram?" and says: "Audio from this channel will be sent to Deepgram, a company outside this building, to be transcribed. Words from your glossary are sent with it. Drafts stay on this computer."
  5. Press Send audio to Deepgram to confirm, or Cancel.
  6. Press Save changes. Until you save, the editor says "When you save, audio from this channel will be sent to Deepgram, outside this building, to be transcribed."

The mock show: choosing Deepgram for the Monitors channel. Nothing is sent anywhere by the mock.

If no key is saved, the confirmation adds: "No Deepgram key is saved yet, so this channel will be transcribed on this computer until you add one."

A copy of a cloud channel (Duplicate) is made as a local channel. Choose cloud again, and confirm again.

The Deepgram key​

Under Deepgram API key a badge says Key saved or No key.

  1. Type the key in Enter the Deepgram key (it reads Replace the Deepgram key if one is saved).
  2. Press Save key. The screen says "Key saved."

"The key is stored on this computer and is never shown again." No browser, not even an admin's, is sent a saved key. See Saved secrets.

Remove key asks "Remove the Deepgram key?" and warns: "Channels set to Deepgram will be transcribed on this computer until a key is saved again."

The mark on a cloud channel​

A channel set to cloud speech carries a small mark wherever it is listed. Hover it for the long form.

MarkMeaning
Cloud"Cloud speech: audio from this channel is sent to Deepgram, outside this building, to be transcribed."
Cloud paused"Cloud speech is paused after failures. Lines are transcribed on this computer; Earshot tests the service with one short piece of audio about every 30 seconds."
On this computerEither "Deepgram rejected the key, so this channel is transcribed on this computer. No audio is sent." or "No key saved, so this channel is transcribed on this computer."

People who cannot read diagnostics (viewers) see only Cloud, meaning "Set to cloud speech: this channel's audio may be sent to Deepgram, outside this building."

The status box​

At the top of the Cloud speech part, a status box gives one of six states.

StatusThe screen says
Not used"No channel is set to cloud speech. Choose “Deepgram (cloud)” as a channel's engine, under Channels."
No key"No key saved, so the channel set to cloud speech is being transcribed on this computer. Save a key below."
Waiting"Set to cloud speech. Nothing has come back from Deepgram yet."
Working"The channel set to cloud speech is sending audio to Deepgram."
Paused"Paused after failures. Lines are transcribed on this computer; Earshot tests the service with one short piece of audio about every 30 seconds."
Key rejected"Deepgram rejected this key. Enter the key again and save it to try once more."

Under it are four counters, "Counted since Earshot started":

CounterCounts
Lines from the cloudLines Deepgram transcribed.
Lines from this computer"Lines from this computer are ones the cloud did not answer, or that were sent there while it was paused."
Audio sentHow much audio has gone out.
FailuresRequests that failed. The most recent reason is shown as Last problem:.

When the service fails​

  • No line is lost. If Deepgram does not answer, the line is transcribed on this computer instead.
  • After repeated failures cloud speech pauses. While it is paused, lines are transcribed on this computer.
  • A little audio still leaves while it is Paused. Earshot sends one short piece of audio about every 30 seconds to test whether the service is back.
  • If Deepgram rejects the key, uploads stop and everything is transcribed on this computer. Saving the key again makes Earshot try again.
  • Cloud speech does not record, line by line, which engine produced a line. The counters are the only tally.

Translation​

Viewers choose a language to read in from the display menu. This part of the screen decides what does the translating and how many languages at once. How it looks to a reader is on Read in another language.

Translate with​

Three cards. The one in use carries an In use now badge. Pick a card, then press Apply (until then it says "Not applied yet.").

CardThe app says
This computer (the default)"Translates with the models installed under Models. Text never leaves this computer."
DeepL"Sends the text of each translated line to DeepL, outside this building. Needs a DeepL key. If DeepL fails, models on this computer are used."
Claude"Sends the text of each translated line to Anthropic, outside this building, to be translated by Claude. Needs an Anthropic key. If Claude fails, models on this computer are used."

Choosing DeepL or Claude asks first, for example "Translate with DeepL?": "The text of each line that is translated will be sent to DeepL, a company outside this building. Audio is not sent." If no key is saved it adds "No DeepL key is saved yet, so DeepL will not be used until you add one below." Press Use DeepL to confirm. The screen then says "Now translating with DeepL."

This computer needs a translation model for each language pair, installed under Setup, Models. German to English and English to Spanish have been run. The other pairs in the list are untested downloads. See Models.

Languages at once​

"How many languages are translated at the same time, for everyone reading. Another language is refused while all of them are in use. 0 turns translation off."

It starts at 4. Type a whole number and press Save. Everyone's choices share this limit: if four people are reading in four different languages, a fifth language is refused and that reader sees the host's own sentence saying so.

Each loaded local pair uses about 330 MB of memory on the host, so a small computer may want a lower number.

Keys for cloud translators​

Two key boxes, DeepL and Anthropic, work exactly like the Deepgram one: a Key saved or No key badge, Save key, Remove key, and "The key is stored on this computer and is never shown again." Removing one warns that the translator "will not be used for translation until a key is saved again".

Right now​

A status box with four counters, "Counted since Earshot started":

CounterCounts
Languages in useThe languages people are reading in at this moment.
Lines translatedTranslations that worked.
Lines that failedLines that could not be translated. If someone picks a language that few lines can reach, the lines that cannot be translated stay in the original and are counted here.
Lines skipped"A line is skipped when translation is busy, and then shows only the original."

If a cloud translator has failed, the box says "The cloud translator is paused after a failure, so it is being skipped for now." and the models on this computer are used meanwhile. The most recent reason is shown as Last problem:.

A reading aid, never a cue call

Show jargon is often mistranslated. In testing, "Sound cue 7, go." came out in Spanish as "Suena a señal 7, vamos." and "kick drum" as "tambor de patada". Automations never see a translation. Do not call a cue from translated text.

Saved audio​

A channel with Keep audio for replay on (Setup, Channels) "saves the audio behind each line on this computer, so anyone who can read that channel can replay it."

SettingStarts atMeaning
Keep for (days)14Clips older than this are deleted.
At most (MB)2048If the total is over this size, the oldest go first.

"0 means no limit." Type whole numbers and press Save (until then it says "Not saved yet.").

  • The limits are checked when Earshot starts, every 10 minutes, when you change them, and as clips are added.
  • Clearing a channel's transcript makes its audio unreachable at once, and the files are deleted.
  • Anyone who can read the channel can replay it. That includes viewers.
  • The audio is kept in the clips folder inside the host's data folder. See Data and files.

How replay looks and behaves for a reader is on Replay a line's audio.

Example: one cloud channel, everything else local​

The FOH Audio channel is hard for the local model, and the production has agreed to send that one channel out.

  1. Setup, Cloud & audio: type the key under Deepgram API key, press Save key. The badge reads Key saved and the status reads Not used.
  2. Setup, Channels, FOH Audio: set Engine to Deepgram (cloud), confirm with Send audio to Deepgram, press Save changes.
  3. Back on Cloud & audio, the status reads Waiting, then Working once a line has come back. Lines from the cloud starts to count.
  4. In the Feed, FOH Audio now carries the Cloud mark. The other seven channels have none: their audio never leaves the computer.
  5. Tell the crew on that channel. Their voices are now going to an outside company.

If the status turns to Paused mid-show, nothing needs doing: lines keep arriving, transcribed locally. If it turns to Key rejected, enter the key again.

Before-show check​

  • Every channel showing a Cloud mark is one you meant, and a key is saved.
  • Translate with is on the card you meant. If it is DeepL or Claude, the production knows the text of lines is leaving the building.
  • Keep for (days) suits the show's rules about recordings.
