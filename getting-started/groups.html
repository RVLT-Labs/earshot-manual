Groups

A group is a named folder of channels, like Stage or Audio. Groups make a long channel list readable, and they let people show a whole department with one press. The Feed and Lanes can filter by group, and an automation can listen to a group instead of naming every channel.

Who can do this

Admins make and edit groups. Everyone sees them.

What a group is, and is not​

  • A group is a static folder. It changes only when an admin edits it under Setup, Groups.
  • Choosing a channel on the Feed never changes a group. Filtering is a choice on your own screen.
  • A channel can be in more than one group, or in none.
  • Deleting a group does not delete its channels.

Make a group​

The mock show's three groups. Not a real show.

  1. Open Setup, then Groups, and press Add.
  2. Under Name and colour, type a Name and pick a Colour.
  3. Tick the channels that belong in it. Find a channel narrows a long list.
  4. Under Order in the group, use the arrows to put the channels in the order you want them listed.
  5. Switch on Pinned if this group should be listed first: "Pinned groups are listed first in the channel rail."
  6. Press Create group.

The mock show's Stage group. Its four channels are listed in the order they appear in the Feed's channel list.

To change a group later, pick it in the list, edit it, and press Save changes. Revert drops changes you have not saved. The arrows beside a group in the list move the group itself up and down.

Worked example: three departments​

The mock show in this manual's pictures has eight channels in three groups:

GroupPinnedChannels
StageYesShow Caller, Stage Manager, Deck Left, Deck Right
AudioNoFOH Audio, Monitors
Lighting & VideoNoLighting, Video

Stage is pinned because it is the one everybody watches. On the Feed, a sound operator presses Audio in the channel list and sees only FOH Audio and Monitors. The stage manager's screen is unaffected.

The mock show's Feed after pressing the Stage group: "4 of 8 channels".

Where groups are used​

PlaceWhat a group does there
FeedPress a group's name in the channel list to pick all its channels.
LanesBy group shows one column per group.
AutomationsWhere to listen can name groups, and a condition can check "Channel is in a group".

Delete a group​

Press the bin beside the group and confirm with Delete group. "The group is removed. Its channels are kept."

If automations listen to that group, Earshot tells you which, under "Used by automations:", and warns that "They will still point at a group that no longer exists." Open each one and choose another group or channel, or the automation will not hear what you expect.
