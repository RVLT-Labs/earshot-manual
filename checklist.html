Before-show checklist

Go through this before every show. It takes a few minutes, and each item links to the page that explains it. Print the page if you like a pen.

danger

Finish with the header showing Safe. Arm only when you mean to, and only after every phrase automation has been tried on the Test bench and said out loud on the real channel.

The host​

  • The host computer is on mains power and will not sleep. If the computer sleeps, Earshot stops listening. (Limits)
  • The header says Live, and shows the show name you expect. (Reading the transcript)
  • The speech models show Installed under Setup, Models. A venue with no internet cannot download them. (Models)
  • You know that a restart of the host starts a new session, signs out everyone who used the password, and comes back Safe. (What happens when the host restarts)

Channels​

  • Every channel shows a level meter that moves when someone speaks, and no No input, Offline or Error badge. (Status and badges)
  • Say a test line on each channel. Read it in the Feed. (Feed)
  • No channel sits on Behind while people are talking normally. (Troubleshooting)
  • If you use voice names: Separate and recognise voices on this channel is on, detected voices have been named or deliberately learned on that channel, and you know names can be wrong. (People)
  • Names, songs and gear that the recogniser gets wrong are in the glossary, and no entry says "Not applied". (Glossary)
  • If you show another computer's channel: its status line says "Linked to …", and you know whether the link is encrypted (it is not, in practice). (Showing another Earshot computer's channels)
  • Any channel set to cloud speech shows its Cloud mark, you meant it, and a key is saved. Cloud speech has never been tried against the real service. (Cloud & audio)
  • Any channel with Keep audio for replay on is one you mean to record, and the people on it know. (Replay a line's audio, Privacy)

The crew​

  • Who can connect is set the way you mean it (Setup, Access). Remember Open means everyone on the network can arm. (Who can connect)
  • Crew have the address from Address for the crew, and each phone shows Live. (Who can connect, Phones and tablets)
  • Anyone who should only read has a Viewer link, not the password. (Invites and personal links, Roles)
  • Old personal links you no longer want are revoked. (Invites and personal links)
  • People who read in another language have picked it under Read in, and know a translation is a reading aid, never a cue call. (Read in another language)

Automations​

  • The header shows Safe. (Safe and Armed)
  • The Automations list does not say "… automation is not running." (When an automation is not running)
  • Every phrase automation has been tried on the Test bench, and said out loud on the real channel while Safe, then checked in Activity ("would have fired"). (Run now, the Test bench and Activity)
  • Number calls have been said the way the caller really says them, and none is refused in Activity. (How Earshot hears numbers)
  • Each automation has a Cooldown that suits it. (Building an automation)
  • Each OSC destination has the right address, port and protocol, and the device is on the network. (Actions)
  • No automation that must go, or must not go for the wrong person, is limited to named people. (Automations limited to people)
  • Start armed is off, unless the show runs unattended and you mean it. (Consoles and controllers)
  • Everyone who can press Make safe knows where it is. Alt+Shift+S works from anywhere on the screen. (Safe and Armed)

Consoles and controllers​

  • If a console will send OSC: OSC input is on, at the lowest level that does the job, with the console's IP address in the list, and arming (if used) over TCP. (OSC input)
  • The console's Make safe button has been pressed once, with the show Armed in rehearsal, and the header went to Safe. (OSC examples)
  • Each console cue that triggers an Earshot automation has been fired once while Safe, and shows in Activity as a dry run. (OSC addresses)
  • MIDI controllers are plugged in, their ports are ticked, and a press shows as "MIDI in" in Activity. (MIDI)
  • Allow automations to run shell commands is off, unless you use shell actions and have read each one. (Consoles and controllers)

After the show​

  • Press Make safe.
  • Export the session if you want a copy outside Earshot (Archive, Export). (Archive and search)
  • Export the show file if you changed channels, automations or the glossary today. (Show files)
  • Revoke links made for guests. (Invites and personal links)
