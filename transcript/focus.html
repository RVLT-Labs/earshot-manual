Focus

Focus shows one channel, or one group, in very large type. It is made for a second screen: a monitor at the prompt desk, a display in the quick-change area, a screen beside the sound desk for someone who cannot wear a headset.

Every role can use Focus.

The mock show "Spring Tour at Harbour Hall". Invented lines, not a real show.

Choose what to show​

  1. Open Focus. It starts on the first channel of the show.
  2. Use the box at the top left to choose. It lists Channels, then Groups.
  • A channel shows only that channel's lines. The channel name is not repeated on every line, because it is in the box.
  • A group merges the lines of the group's channels, and each line says which channel it came from.
  • Until something is said, the screen reads "Waiting for" and the name.

The top bar also carries the channel's level meter, its badge if it needs a look, and the Cloud mark if the channel is set to cloud speech.

Each choice has its own address, so a screen can be set to open straight on it. For a channel the address ends #/focus/c/ followed by the channel's short name, for example #/focus/c/show-caller. Bookmark it on the machine that drives the second screen. If the channel is later deleted or its short name changes, the screen says "That channel was not found in this show. Choose another from the list."

How big the text is​

The type is sized to the window: a bigger window gives bigger type. Your Text size setting (Display on this device) scales it further, from 85% to 175%. Draft lines are grey and final lines are solid, as everywhere else.

Focus keeps the newest lines on screen (the last six finished lines, and whatever is being said now) and lets older ones go. It is for the call that is happening now. To read back, use the Feed or Lanes.

Pop out, Keep on top, Full screen​

ButtonWhat it does
Pop outOpens this Focus in its own window, without the rest of Earshot around it. Drag that window to the second screen.
Keep on topIn the desktop app's pop-out window only. The desktop app opens a pop-out already kept above other windows, so the button starts switched on. Press it to let other windows cover it.
Full screenFills the screen. Press it again (Exit full screen), or press Esc, to come back. It is offered only where the browser allows full screen.

In a browser, Pop out opens a new browser window. If the browser blocks it you see "The pop-out window was blocked. Allow pop-ups for this page and try again." The new window is signed in as you are.

You can also get to Focus from Lanes: a lane's menu has Pop out to Focus.

warning

In the desktop app, pop-out windows and Keep on top have not been tried by hand in this build. Try them on your own machine before you rely on them for a show. See Limits and known behaviour.

A worked example: a call screen in the quick-change room​

The dressers on the mock show need to see the show caller's calls and cannot wear headsets.

  1. On the laptop that drives the quick-change room's screen, open the address the operator gave you (see Who can connect).
  2. Open Focus and choose Show Caller.
  3. Press Pop out, drag the new window to the big screen, and press Full screen.
  4. Open the display button and raise Text size until the calls can be read from the far wall.
  5. On that laptop, switch off sleep and the screen saver. If the laptop sleeps, the screen stops updating.

To show the whole deck instead, choose the Stage group in step 2. Each line then carries its channel name.

Driving Focus from a console​

A lighting desk or controller can change what the host computer's own window shows, with OSC: select a channel by name, step to the next or previous channel, scroll. Selecting a channel while the host's window is on the Feed (with all channels showing) or on Lanes switches it to Focus on that channel.

These messages only ever move the Earshot window on the host computer itself. They are never sent to phones, tablets or other computers, and they do nothing while someone at the host is in Setup, Automations, Archive or Activity. See OSC addresses.

Limits​

  • Focus shows live lines from the current session. To read an earlier session, use the Archive.
  • Focus is for reading. To type a line, use the Feed.
  • A screen is only as awake as its computer. Earshot cannot stop a laptop sleeping.
