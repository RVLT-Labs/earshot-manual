Replay a line's audio

Earshot normally turns audio into text and keeps only the text. A channel can instead keep the audio behind each spoken line, so a call can be played back: "what exactly did the show caller say there?". The audio is saved on the host computer and nowhere else.

It is off for every channel until an admin switches it on for that channel.

info

Who can do what. An admin switches saving on, per channel, and sets the limits. After that, anyone who can read the channel can replay it, viewers included.

Switch it on for a channel​

  1. Open Setup, then Channels, and choose the channel.
  2. Switch on Keep audio for replay. The app says: "Saves the audio behind each line on the host computer so anyone who can read this channel can replay it."
  3. Press Save changes.

Only lines spoken after you switch it on have audio. Lines from before have none.

Think about who is on the channel before you do this. Saved audio is a recording of people's voices. Tell the crew, and see Privacy.

Play a line​

A line from a channel that keeps audio gets a small play control at its right edge. Its name is "Play the audio of" followed by the channel and the time, for example "Play the audio of Show Caller, 19:42:07".

  • It appears a moment after the line, because the audio is saved after the line is written.
  • It works in Feed, Lanes, Focus and the Archive.
  • Press it to play. Press it again to pause ("Pause the audio of…"), and again to carry on ("Resume the audio of…").
  • One line plays at a time. Starting another stops the first.
  • The sound comes out of the device you pressed it on, not out of the host.

Lines with no play control​

The lineWhy
A typed lineIt was never audio.
A line from a channel with Keep audio for replay offNothing was saved.
A line spoken before the switch was turned onNothing was saved then.
A line from another Earshot computer (a linked channel)Only text crosses a link, never audio.
A draft lineThe control comes with the finished line.

When the audio has gone​

The control saysWhat happened
"This audio is no longer kept."The limits below deleted it, or the transcript was cleared.
"This audio could not be loaded."The device could not fetch it. Check the connection and try again.
"This audio could not be played."The device fetched it but could not play it.

How long audio is kept​

Open Setup, then Cloud & audio, and find Saved audio. Two limits apply to all channels together:

SettingStarts atMeaning
Keep for (days)14Clips older than this are deleted. 0 means no age limit.
At most (MB)2048The most disk space clips may use. When the total is over, the oldest go first. 0 means no size limit.

Type the numbers and press Save.

  • The limits are checked when Earshot starts, every 10 minutes, when you change them, and as clips are added.
  • The clips live in the clips folder inside Earshot's data folder on the host. See Where data and files live.
  • With both limits at 0, clips are kept until you clear the transcript or delete the folder. On a long run that can fill the disk.

Clearing a transcript​

Clearing a transcript makes its audio unreachable at once, and deletes the files. A clip that finishes being written after its line was cleared is deleted and never offered.

An automation can clear a transcript, and so can a console at the Full control level. See Actions and OSC addresses.

A worked example: checking a disputed call​

After the mock show's first preview, lighting and the show caller disagree about what was called at the top of act two.

Before the show, the admin opened Setup, Channels, Show Caller, switched on Keep audio for replay and saved. Under Cloud & audio the limits were left at 14 days and 2048 MB.

After the show:

  1. Open Archive and pick tonight's session.
  2. Type act two in Search what was said. Press the matching Show Caller line.
  3. Press its play control and listen.
  4. Play the lines either side of it, one at a time.

The lighting operator, who is a viewer on a personal link, can do exactly the same from their own phone.

Limits​

  • Replay was checked against the real host, but nobody has yet confirmed that it is audible on a phone or tablet speaker. Try it on the devices your crew will use.
  • Only finished spoken lines have audio. There is no continuous recording: what falls between lines is not kept. The app has no button for saving a line's audio as a file.
  • Saved audio is never sent to another Earshot computer, and is never part of a show file or a transcript export.
